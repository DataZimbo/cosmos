The holding entity over everything is **Amor Mundi** — Hannah Arendt's *amor
mundi*, "love of the world." It does not run the companies directly; it holds,
funds, and integrates them.

## 2. Charis [sp?] — the family office

The **Bessemer-style family office**, carrying a Greek name — **Charis**
(χάρις, grace) **[sp?]** — described as *born out of* **Elpis [sp?]** (ἐλπίς,
hope — what remained in Pandora's jar). It was always *meant to expand.*

Its charge is the family, in full: **psychological, financial, and spiritual
fulfillment** — the human center of the whole structure.

---

## 3. Munzu Capital — the capital engine

The disciplined capital core. Its mandate:

> **Preserve, expand, and deepen capital — in all its many forms — along with
> influence and, most importantly, access.**

The posture is non-retreating: *"We do not move back from that, after
everything that's been sacrificed."*

---

## 4. Chirandu — the builder

Named for the **Shona totem** (*Moyo Chirandu* — the heart). Run by **the
Triumphant**. Modeled on the **"Immortal Company,"** with two portions and a
deliberately limited move-set inside each:

### 4a. Exploit — the "one-plus" companies
- Mature companies that have found what works; **one → plus.**
- Led by a **CEO**.
- Objectives: **avoid obsolescence; grow profits and revenue.**

### 4b. Explore — zero to one
- The search phase; **zero → one.**
- Led by a **Chief Innovation Officer**.
- Objectives: **reduce risk and raise the probability of landing the huge
  idea.**

There are **limited moves within each portion** — a limited number of rooms,
therefore a limited number of ways to fail, *if disciplined.*

### 4c. What unifies both — the innovation taxonomy
1. **Market-creating innovation** — number one, the kind that builds new
   markets.
2. **Sustaining innovation** — making good products better.
3. **Efficiency innovation** — doing more with less.
4. *(A fourth category was recalled but not named in dictation — possibly
   "maintaining innovation." Confirm in §9.)*

---

## 5. Rozvi Research — special circumstances

Named for the **Rozvi Empire** — *the destroyers* (from Shona *kurozva*).
Handles **special circumstances**.

**Doctrine — F.E.A.R.:** **F**irst **E**ncounter **A**ssessment
**R**econnaissance (or **R**esearch). Small, unnamed teams that make first
contact with the unknown and assess it.

**If something proves to be a threat**, two response postures:

- **Secure · Contain · Protect** — lock it down and neutralize exposure.
- **Embrace · Extend · Extinguish** — absorb it, outgrow it, end it.

---

## 6. Soma Sciences — long-term influence

**Soma Sciences** — *soma* as in the **mushroom**; the **mycelium** network
that runs beneath everything and ties it together. Runs the **longer-term
influence campaigns** — patient, deep-horizon plays measured in years, not
quarters.
