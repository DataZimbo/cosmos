## 2. Ranchero — the family office

Modeled on a **Bessemer-style** ("Bismar") family office. Its charge is the
**family**, in full:

- **psychological, financial, and spiritual fulfillment** — the human center
  of the whole structure.

Everything else exists to serve and be stewarded through this.

---

## 3. Munzu Capital — the capital engine

The disciplined capital core. Its mandate, in its own words:

> **Preserve, expand, and deepen capital — in all its many forms — along with
> influence and, most importantly, access.**

The posture is non-retreating: *"We do not move back from that, after
everything that's been sacrificed."* This is the long-horizon, keep-and-compound
engine.

*(Open item: Ranchero and Munzu Capital are split here into "the family's
needs" vs. "the capital mandate." The dictation blended the two under the
"first one." If they should be a single entity, collapse §2 and §3 — see §8.)*

---

## 4. Truandu — the builder

Run by **Triverent [sp?]**. Modeled on the **"Immortal Company."** It has two
portions:

- **Explore** — **zero → one.** Standing up something new; finding the thing.
- **Exploit** — **one → plus.** Compounding what already works.

The structural discipline: there are a **limited number of rooms** — therefore a
**limited number of ways to fail**, *provided you stay disciplined.* Scarcity of
slots is the feature, not the constraint.
