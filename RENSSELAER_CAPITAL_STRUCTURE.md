## 1. The holding entity

A corporation that runs other corporations — a **harness**. It does not
operate businesses directly; it holds, directs, and integrates them.

The operating companies sit under it across sectors, "from cattle to
technology." Names captured from dictation (spellings to confirm):

- **Manchuro [sp?]**
- **Zoo / Churandu [sp?]**
- **Rosvi [sp?]**
- **Soma** — see §4.

The integrating identity for the whole system is **Pando** — after the
clonal aspen colony that is a single connected organism beneath the
surface. The metaphor is deliberate: **mycelium**. The visible companies
are fruiting bodies; the value and the connective tissue live in the
network underneath, keeping everything integrated into one system.

---

## 2. Rensselaer Capital — the private-wealth branch

Rensselaer Capital is the **private wealth management** arm — the family
office. Reference model: **Bessemer** (Bessemer Trust).

**Mandate:** long-term investments, strategy, and social capital —
"the 5 / 50 / 500" tiers of influence.

**Ethos:** it *maintains*, it *preserves*, it **compounds conservatively.**

Structurally it has **three heads but two parts**:

### 2a. The Exploit group
Companies that have **already found product–market fit.** There is a
*finite* number of transformations available to carry each one forward —
a limited set of moves to protect a proven business from **redundancy and
disruption** ("innovation" turning against it). The job is to extract and
extend durable value from what already works.

### 2b. The Explore group
The opposite posture: **velocity of experiments.** Run many, get past the
dead ends fast, and either **find product–market fit or acquire /
replicate it.** This is the search function that feeds graduates into the
Exploit group once fit is established.

---

## 3. Roosevelt Research — reconnaissance & talent

**Roosevelt Research** is the forward, first-contact function —
**FEBA [sp?]**, in the "first encounter and assault reconnaissance"
sense. Small, **unnamed teams** that "just handle business."

Two recurring capabilities:

- **Clean-room reverse engineering** — understanding and rebuilding
  capability from the outside in.
- **Talent pipeline** — recruiting the next **"germlines"**: young
  scientists, developed the way pharmaceutical companies cultivate early
  researchers. Building the bench for the next cycle.

People move between the transit/operating side, the research side, and
the clean-room side depending on where they're strongest.

---

## 4. Soma — the long-term core

**Soma** is named after the **mushroom** — long-term holdings and ties,
the **mycelium** that ties back to the **Pando** identity in §1.

This is the **secretive** part of the system. Its mandate is preservation
above all:

- **Capital** must be preserved.
- **Reputation** must be preserved.
- The **axis** must be preserved — and **expanded and compounded.**

The stated temperament is patient, quiet, compounding capital.
*"Charlie Munger would be proud."*
